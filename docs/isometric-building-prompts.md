# Isometric Building Image Prompts (Medieval Fantasy)

Each image should be: **isometric view, medieval fantasy style, slightly cartoonish, high quality, transparent/plain white background**.

## 1. Town Hall
- **Kind:** `townHall`
- **Description:** Center of settlement governance. Large, multi-story civic building with a bell tower.
- **Prompt:**
  > Isometric pixel-art-style game asset of a medieval fantasy town hall. Large two-story stone civic hall with a tall central clock tower, slate roof, arched entrance with double wooden doors, ornate window frames, flying pennants on the tower, warm stone brick texture, slightly cartoonish proportions, high quality render, isolated on plain white background, no shadows on ground.
